# Yemen District Data Source

Dataset:
Yemen Administrative Divisions Dataset

Source:
https://github.com/open-admin-data/yemen-administrative-divisions

Published dataset version:
2026.09

License:
CC BY 4.0

Original data:
22 governorates and 335 districts.

Usage:
Independent YNDR research prototype.

Important:
Administrative records require independent review.
Generated gov.ye domains are proposals only.
They do not indicate domain registration,
government authorization or ownership.
